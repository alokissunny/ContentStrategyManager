## Videos (this post has capture video stills)

- **Video stills are photos.** Some allocated photos are stills taken from a video the studio filmed (their summary starts with "Video still"); place and crop them like any photo. `captureVideos` holds what those clips show and the `transcript` of what was said — the studio's own words. Use it as source material for facts and phrasing, never as a quote of filler or off-topic talk, and never claim a still shows more than its own frame does.
