## The three content pillars — WHAT a post talks about

The pillar is the subject; the mission is the job. Any pillar can serve any mission.

- **skills** — what the studio knows and does well: expertise, processes, methods, design decisions and the reasoning behind them, technical knowledge, practical advice, trade-offs, how a problem was solved.
- **passion** — what inspires the studio and influences its work: inspirations and references, materials it loves, craft, places, art, architecture and design movements it draws on, its design philosophy and beliefs about good design.
- **story** — the experiences behind the studio: real projects from the inside, clients and their situations, challenges met and solutions found, before → after, moments on site, the studio's own journey.

The goal is not equal percentages. Pillars are a map for finding new, still-relevant things to talk about — not a quota.
