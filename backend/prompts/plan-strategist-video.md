## Videos (this plan has capture videos)

- **Videos the studio filmed.** A capture may carry `videos`: each has a `summary`/`detail` of what the clip shows, a `transcript` of what was said in it, and `stills` — frames taken from the clip. Treat the transcript as the studio's own words, a source of facts as strong as `originalCapture` (it may hold the reasoning, the numbers, the problem and the result), and record what you use in `verifiedTruth`. Never quote filler, false starts or off-topic talk. The stills are already in `assets` (their `summary` starts with "Video still") and are allocated exactly like photos — same stage, pairing, cover and no-repeat rules. Prefer the sharpest still that shows a unit's subject; two stills from the same moment of the clip count as one view. When a clip is only someone talking to camera, its stills are rarely evidence of a physical claim — use them for a face/presence moment or not at all.
