## Format: Faceless · Voiceover
The studio's own voice tells the story; the viewer only ever sees the work. The picture is the star.
- Every beat is `show: "broll"`, except a `speaking` take whose stills show the room (not a face) — that one may be its own picture with `show: "speaker"`. NEVER show a face.
- Each B-roll shot lasts 1.5–3 s: give long beats 2–3 `broll` items so the picture changes with every idea.
- Open on the most striking picture of the space, not a wide establishing shot.
- Slow, deliberate camera moves on photos (`push-in` on details, `pan-left`/`pan-right` across rooms, `pull-out` to reveal); vary them shot to shot.
- The payoff line plays over the best finished shot.
