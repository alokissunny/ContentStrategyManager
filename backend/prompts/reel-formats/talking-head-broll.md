## Format: Talking head + B-roll (the explainer)
The studio explains a decision on camera; B-roll proves every point. This is the highest-trust format for "position" and "convert" content — it must look edited, not recorded.
- Hook (first beat): speaker on screen, `zoom: medium`, the hook line said straight to camera. No B-roll in the first 1.5 s.
- From the second beat on, B-roll covers 45–65 % of the running time. Every time they NAME a thing (a material, a room, a constraint, a result) cut to it.
- Alternate: speaker → cutaway → speaker. Never more than ~6 s of uninterrupted face after the hook; never more than ~8 s of uninterrupted B-roll (bring the face back to re-anchor trust).
- When the speaker returns after a cutaway, change the framing (`wide` ↔ `medium` ↔ `close`) so it reads as a new shot, not a jump.
- Use `close` only for the single most important sentence (the reason or the payoff).
- The payoff beat ends on B-roll of the result, then the end card.
- If there is no B-roll at all, still vary framing every beat and put `label`s on the turning points.
