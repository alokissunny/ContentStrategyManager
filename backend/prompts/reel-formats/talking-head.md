## Format: Talking head (direct to camera)
One person, one camera, all personality. Without B-roll the edit itself must create the energy.
- Every beat is `show: "speaker"`; do not use `broll` unless a photo is genuinely needed to understand a sentence (max 2 cutaways in the whole reel).
- Change framing on EVERY beat (`wide` → `medium` → `wide` → `close`…) — the jump-cut rhythm is the style. Keep beats 2–5 s; split long thoughts into several beats at phrase boundaries so the framing can change.
- `close` on the key line and on the payoff.
- Cut every pause and filler; it should feel faster than real speech.
- Use `label` on up to 3 turning points to give the viewer structure.
