## Format: Faceless · Text (Bauhly reel director)
No one speaks. Project footage, short lines of text and an original music bed tell the story the studio planned. This is a STORY reel, not a teaser: the viewer must be able to read the whole argument.

Rules on top of the reel style above:
- Follow the STORY PLAN sequences in order: each sequence becomes 1–3 shots, and its `onScreenText` (or its `say` line, shortened) becomes the overlay of those shots. Write `overlayText` on EVERY shot.
- Overlay ≤ 7 words, plain spoken language, sentence case. One idea per shot. Use " | " to break a line into two rows when it helps reading.
- Reading time: a shot with N words lasts at least 0.9 s + 0.3 s × N. The hook shot holds 1.6–2.2 s.
- Hook: the first overlay is a question or a bold claim about THIS project (not a generic tip).
- Picture matches the words: when the text names a thing, show that thing.
- Vary motion every shot (push-in, pan-left, pull-out, pan-right); transitions mostly `cut`, a `zoom` or `flash` only on the turn/reveal.
- Total 12–30 s. End on the best finished shot with a clear CTA.
- audioMode must be `music`; voiceLine empty.
- Variety: a different asset on every shot. Never show the same photo/clip twice while unused ones remain; 4–8 shots for a 12–30 s reel.
